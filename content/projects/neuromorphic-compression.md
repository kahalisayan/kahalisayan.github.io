## Overview

Neuromorphic approaches to data compression, carried out at TCS Research &
Innovation. The SPIKECODE line of work uses spiking neural networks for low-power,
lossless image compression suited to small-satellite edge communication and other
constrained-hardware settings.

## Motivation

Edge and satellite systems have tight power and bandwidth budgets. Spiking neural
networks offer an energy-efficient computing model; applying them to lossless
compression can reduce data-transmission volume while keeping reconstruction exact.

## Research questions

- Can spiking neural networks perform lossless image compression within edge power
  budgets?
- How can the approach be made hardware-agnostic across neuromorphic platforms?
- What are the trade-offs between compression, reconstruction speed, and energy use?

## Approach

Spiking-network compression models, low-power design for satellite edge
communication, and hardware-aware deployment on constrained systems.
