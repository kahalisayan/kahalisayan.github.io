## Overview

AI for colonoscopy that fits into the clinical workflow rather than around it.
Topics under study include disease-activity assessment, scorable-frame selection,
video understanding, polyp segmentation, and uncertainty-aware prediction.

## Motivation

Colonoscopy video is long, variable in quality, and only partly informative for any
given assessment. Selecting scorable frames and providing consistent, confidence-aware
readouts can reduce variability and support clinical decisions.

## Research questions

- Which frames in a colonoscopy video are actually scorable, and how can they be
  identified automatically?
- How can disease-activity assessment be made consistent and confidence-aware?
- How should model uncertainty be communicated within the reading workflow?

## Approach

Video-level modelling, frame-selection methods, segmentation, and uncertainty-aware
prediction, evaluated with clinical workflow support in mind.

## Status

Ongoing and unpublished. Described here at the level of direction only, without
patient data or internal results.
