## Undergraduate teaching

As Assistant Professor in Computer Science & Engineering at Siliguri Institute of
Technology (2009–2015), I taught the following undergraduate subjects:

- Operating Systems
- Computer Organization
- Computer Graphics
- Object-Oriented Programming with Java

## Mentoring

Alongside research, I have supervised and mentored student projects in computer
science and, more recently, in medical AI. I am happy to discuss project supervision
and mentoring with prospective students and collaborators.
